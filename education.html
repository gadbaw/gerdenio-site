Education

Learning in community.

Alongside the clinical work, the Center offers free webinars and teaching events for clinicians, trainees, students, and anyone curious about depth-oriented psychotherapy. Sessions are live, conversational, and recorded.

Upcoming Webinar

Tuesday, August 25, 2026 · 12:15–1:45 PM Pacific

The Psychodynamic Capacities: Making Depth Therapy Teachable

Max Levine, Psy.D.

FreeLive on Zoom90 minutesRecorded

Max Levine, Psy.D. presents the teaching model he has been developing for making relational psychodynamic therapy concrete and learnable: twelve psychodynamic capacities, the skills that cultivate them, and their translation into motivational-interviewing-compatible ambivalence formulations.

He'll also preview his current work on relational stances — crafting the therapy relationship itself to meet a patient's particular capacities. Ninety minutes, with time for discussion. Free and open to clinicians, trainees, and students. Can't join live? Register anyway — the session will be recorded.

Why We Teach

Passing the work forward.

Psychotherapy is learned the way it is practiced — in relationship. Our founder spent a career teaching and supervising the next generation of clinicians, and the Center continues that commitment: making the deep traditions of the field clear, practical, and shareable.
