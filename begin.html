If in-network coverage is essential, our clinicians also practice at Equity Health, a community health center in San Francisco that accepts Medi-Cal, Medicare, and many insurance plans, with a sliding fee program for the uninsured. What we ask, and why

Format, schedule, and fee: the practical questions that decide whether we can work together. What brings you here can wait for the conversation.
